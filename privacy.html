Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

1. Who we are

StreamSnatch is owned and operated by Gerald Buchanan in California, USA. This policy explains how the StreamSnatch website (https://streamsnatcher.app) and web app (https://app.streamsnatcher.app) ("StreamSnatch," "we," "us") collect, use, store, share, and delete information, including information we receive from Google APIs. Contact: streamsnatchergod@gmail.com.

2. What StreamSnatch does

StreamSnatch turns Twitch and Kick streams from creators who allow clipping into vertical 9:16 Shorts with captions, hook titles, and an estimated virality score. If you choose to connect a YouTube channel, StreamSnatch uploads only the clips you select to that channel.

3. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, a salted hash of your password (never the password itself), your plan, and your credit balance and history.
  • Billing information: if you buy a paid plan, payment is handled by Stripe. We receive your subscription status and plan. We never see or store your card number.
  • YouTube information (only if you connect a channel): your channel ID, channel name, channel avatar URL, and the OAuth access and refresh tokens Google issues to StreamSnatch. See section 4.
  • Content you create: clip files, captions, hook titles, thumbnails, virality scores, upload history, and settings.
  • Usage and technical information: server logs (such as request time, IP address, and errors), job history, and the links you submit for clipping.

4. Google user data (YouTube Data API)

StreamSnatch uses YouTube API Services. If you connect a YouTube channel, StreamSnatch requests only these OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — to upload a video to your channel only after you pick a clip, choose the channel, and press Upload, and to set the title, description, tags, and public, private, or scheduled status you reviewed in the app.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly — to read your channel's ID, name, and avatar so the app can show which channel is connected and confirm where a clip will be uploaded.

StreamSnatch does not read your existing videos, comments, subscribers, analytics, or any other Google account data, and never asks for your Google password (sign-in happens on Google's own page).

5. How we use, store, and protect Google user data

  • Tokens and channel profile data are used only to show your connected channel and to perform uploads you request.
  • They are stored on StreamSnatch's private server in the United States, are sent only over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and can be accessed only by the app and its operator.
  • At least every 30 days, StreamSnatch checks with Google that it is still authorized and refreshes your channel name and avatar. If access was revoked or the channel is gone, that data is deleted.

6. How we share information

We do not sell personal information or Google user data, and we do not use them for advertising. StreamSnatch shows no third-party ads. We share information only:

  • With YouTube (Google), when you upload a clip: the video file and the title, description, tags, and privacy settings you chose are sent to YouTube so it can appear on your channel.
  • With service providers that run StreamSnatch for us: Stripe (payments), Cloudflare (secure network connection to the app), and GitHub Pages (hosting this website). They process limited data, such as IP addresses or billing details, only to provide their services. Google user data is not shared with them.
  • When required by law, to protect against fraud, abuse, or security threats, or as part of a merger or sale of StreamSnatch, in which case we will ask for your consent before Google user data is transferred.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

StreamSnatch's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google user data is used only to provide the user-facing features described above.
  • It is not transferred to others except as needed to provide those features, for security, to comply with law, or in a merger or acquisition with your prior consent.
  • It is not used or transferred for serving ads, including retargeting, personalized, or interest-based advertising, and it is never sold.
  • No person reads it unless you give us permission (for example, to help with a support request), it is needed for security (for example, investigating abuse or a bug), or it is required by law.
  • It is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

8. YouTube and Google terms

By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

9. Cookies and data on your device

StreamSnatch places one essential cookie in your browser to keep you signed in. The installable web app uses a service worker that caches only the app's own layout files and icons so it opens quickly. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. Stripe sets its own cookies on its checkout page to process payments and prevent fraud. You can clear these at any time in your browser settings; you will then need to sign in again.

10. Stream content

To make clips, StreamSnatch temporarily downloads parts of the Twitch or Kick streams and videos you choose to clip. You may only clip content you own or that the creator allows clipping.

11. Retention and deletion

  • YouTube connection: kept until you disconnect the channel or revoke access. When you click Remove in the app, StreamSnatch revokes its access with Google and deletes the tokens and channel profile data right away. If you revoke access from Google's settings instead, we delete that data within 7 days of detecting the revocation.
  • Clips: Free plan clips are deleted after 3 days, Starter after 29 days, and Pro clips are kept until you delete them.
  • Account: kept while your account is open. When you delete your account (Plan tab → Delete my account), we cancel any paid subscription, revoke and delete your YouTube connections, and delete your account, clips, and jobs right away. Requests by email are completed within 7 days. Billing records Stripe must keep by law are kept by Stripe.
  • Logs: kept only as long as needed to operate and secure the app.

Deleting data in StreamSnatch does not delete videos already uploaded to YouTube. To remove those, use YouTube or YouTube Studio.

12. Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect YouTube: on the YouTube (or Accounts) tab, click Remove next to the channel.
  • Revoke access from Google: in addition to disconnecting in the app, you can revoke StreamSnatch's access at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Delete your account: in the app, open the Plan tab and choose Delete my account, or email streamsnatchergod@gmail.com from your account's email address and we will delete it within 7 days.
  • Get a copy of your data: email streamsnatchergod@gmail.com from your account's email address.
  • California residents have the right to know, delete, and correct personal information and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

13. Children

StreamSnatch is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information or Google user data from children under 13.

14. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date. If we start using Google user data in a new way, we will ask you to accept the updated policy before continuing.

15. Contact

Questions or complaints about this policy or our privacy practices: streamsnatchergod@gmail.com.
