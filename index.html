Live-stream clipping

Turn live streams into ready-to-post Shorts.

StreamSnatch watches Twitch, Kick, and YouTube, cuts highlight moments into vertical 9:16 clips with modern captions and hook titles, scores each clip with an estimated virality percentage, and can upload the ones you choose to your own YouTube channel.

Log in / Create account See pricing

Track live streams

Follow streamers on Twitch and Kick, get live alerts, and clip from YouTube videos you paste in. Built for categories like GTA, Fortnite, and roleplay servers.

Auto-cut viral-style clips

While a stream is live, StreamSnatch finds highlight moments and renders Shorts no longer than 30 seconds, filed by streamer and category.

Captions, hooks, and score

Each clip gets word-by-word captions, a hook status line, punch-in zooms, sound effects, and an estimated virality score so you can pick the strongest moments.

Upload to your YouTube

Connect your own YouTube channel and publish chosen clips as Shorts. Instagram and TikTok stay download-only. You stay in control of what gets posted.

Pricing

Start free. Credits reset every month. 1 credit = 1 minute of source video (videos under a minute use 1 credit; partial minutes round down). Credits are checked before a job starts and refunded automatically if it fails.
